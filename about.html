About Helping Hand Haven

Our History

Helping Hand Haven was founded in 2000 by Luyanda Prince Shandu, who wished to give underprivileged children a place they could truly call home. Rather than functioning as an adoption agency, Helping Hand Haven provides long-term residential care for children and runaways who have not been adopted, raising them together as siblings.

It all began with one belief: a small gesture of a stranger's helping hand can change the course of a child's life.

Our Mission

To give underprivileged children and runaways a safe, loving home — offering shelter, food, education, and a family-like environment where they grow up as siblings, cared for like they would be by parents.

Our Vision

A world where no child feels forgotten or unwanted — where every underprivileged child has access to a home, an education, and people who care for them.
