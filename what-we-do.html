What We Do

Three pillars support every child at Helping Hand Haven: a home, an education, and consistent care.

A Home, Not Just a Shelter

Children at Helping Hand Haven live together as siblings, in a warm and stable environment. This isn't a temporary stopover — it's a long-term home with routines, rules, and love, just like any household.

Education That Opens Doors

Every child is enrolled in school and supported with homework, tutoring, and school supplies. We believe education is the clearest path out of hardship and into opportunity.

Care That Feels Like Family

Our caregivers provide the guidance, structure, and affection every child needs — emotional support included, not just physical needs met.

What Every Child Receives

  • A bed, meals, and clothing
  • Full-time schooling and tutoring support
  • Access to healthcare
  • Emotional support and counselling when needed
  • A sense of belonging as part of a family
